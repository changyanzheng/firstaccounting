About Us

Ting Li Global Advisory provides practical business guidance and administrative support to help companies operate more efficiently and confidently. We focus on offering clear insights, organised documentation processes, and tailored advisory solutions that assist clients in making informed decisions and strengthening their business operations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver the most accurate, reliable, and high-quality advisory support to businesses of all sizes. We aim to empower companies with clear insights, well-organised processes, and strategic guidance that enhance decision-making and operational confidence.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted advisory partner for both local and international entrepreneurs who wish to explore opportunities in Malaysia. We strive to build a supportive environment where businesses from around the world can receive dependable guidance and a seamless experience as they grow in the Malaysian market.

Our Story

Ting Li Global Advisory was founded with the belief that businesses deserve clarity, structure, and support to operate smoothly—especially those entering a new market. Over time, we recognised that many entrepreneurs, including those coming to Malaysia from overseas, need precise guidance to navigate business processes effectively. With this purpose, we dedicated ourselves to providing the best quality advisory service, helping companies stay organised, informed, and confident as they build their presence in Malaysia.
