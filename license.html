Federal & Agency Approvals

Industry-Specific Licenses

Beyond standard premise permits, specific industries require "License to Operate" from federal ministries. We categorize these into 6 key sectors for your reference.

🏗️

Manufacturing & Construction

Manufacturing License MIDA

Mandatory for factories with shareholders' funds >RM2.5M or >75 staff.

Exemption Letter MIDA

Proof of exemption for smaller factories not meeting ML criteria.

Contractor Registration CIDB

Grades G1-G7. Mandatory for all construction projects.

Machinery Cert (PMA) DOSH

For cranes, lifts, and pressure vessels.

🛍️

Trade, Retail & Distribution

WRT License KPDN

Required for foreign-owned (>51%) retail, F&B, and wholesale businesses.

Direct Selling (AJL) KPDN

Strictly required for all Multi-Level Marketing (MLM) companies.

CSA Control Goods KPDN

For trading sugar, cooking oil, flour, and LPG.

PDA License KPDN

For petrol stations and petroleum product logistics.

🩺

Services & Professional

Clinic (Borang A/B) MOH

Private Medical & Dental Clinics (CKAPS).

Private Education MOE

Kindergartens, Tuition Centers, and International Schools.

Tourism (TOBTAB) MOTAC

Inbound/Outbound travel agencies and ticketing.

Money Services (MSB) BNM

Money changers and remittance services.

🚢

Logistics & Commodities

Operator License APAD

For commercial logistics companies (Lorry/Bus).

Customs Agent JKDM

Forwarding agents handling customs clearance.

Palm Oil (MPOB) MPOB

Planting, milling, transport, and trading of palm oil.

📡

Tech, Media & Telco

NSP / NFP / ASP MCMC

Telcos, cloud providers, and infrastructure builders.

Courier Service MCMC

Last-mile delivery and postal services.

Film Production FINAS

Commercial filming, documentaries, and distribution.

MD Status MDEC

Malaysia Digital status for tax incentives (Tech companies).

🔒

Controlled Activities

Private Security KDN

Security guard agencies (High barrier to entry).

Liquor License District

Retail and Wholesale alcohol sales permits.

Secondhand Dealer PDRM

Scrap metal and recycling businesses.
